ANWAR / Privacy policy

Privacy policy

This page explains what happens when you browse Anwar's portfolio or choose to contact him. The site is a static portfolio and does not provide account creation or server-side enquiry storage.

Information submitted through the homepage form

Information submitted through the homepage form

The homepage enquiry form uses the name, requested video format and project details you enter to prepare a WhatsApp message. Submitting the form navigates your browser to a WhatsApp link containing that prefilled text, where you can review and send it. The website does not submit the form to a site-owned database or retain its field values. The destination URL may be visible in browser history and is handled by WhatsApp under its privacy terms.

Email and WhatsApp links

Email and WhatsApp links

Choosing an email or WhatsApp link opens the relevant service with Anwar's public contact details. Information you send is handled by that service and by Anwar as the recipient, subject to their applicable terms and your communications.

Hosting and external resources

Hosting and external resources

The site is deployed on Vercel. The pages request the Inter font from Google Fonts where that resource is used. Hosting and font providers may process connection data such as an IP address to deliver their services; consult their current privacy notices for details.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The site includes Google's AdSense script to verify site ownership for publisher account ca-pub-3376684105322240. The portfolio's own advertisement placements are disabled by default, and no separate ad unit is configured in the site code. Google may process connection or browser data when this external script is requested; see Google's current privacy information for details. The theme toggle changes the page appearance in the current browser session and does not save a preference.

Contact and updates

Contact and updates

For a privacy question, contact Anwar at anwaransarijmt1098@gmail.com. This policy should be reviewed by the website owner for accuracy and local legal requirements before publication.
